/01   Personal profile

Between legal principle and organisational reality.

Clarity, discretion and sound judgment matter most when the questions are difficult.

My professional background combines legal training, data protection, corporate governance and operational experience. I have worked across legal and business functions, European organisations and the governance structures of a global financial institution.

My work in privacy and enterprise governance is informed by practical experience in corporate administration, international logistics and cost control. This background helps me consider regulatory requirements within the realities of how organisations operate.

I am particularly interested in how regulation becomes workable governance: defining accountability, assessing risk, documenting decisions and aligning legal, technology and business stakeholders. I value precise analysis, clear communication and measures proportionate to the issue at hand.

/04   Professional perspective

Professional perspective

A sound governance framework should be legally grounded, operationally clear and proportionate to the risk.

I regard privacy, data and AI governance as connected disciplines. Legal requirements establish the framework, while sound implementation depends on clear accountability, proportionate controls and an understanding of how organisations make and execute decisions.
